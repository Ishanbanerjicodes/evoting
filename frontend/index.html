How it works

Three steps from registration to result

A hybrid architecture — MySQL for fast, familiar account management; Ethereum for a vote record nobody can quietly edit.

1

Register & verify

Create an account with your voter ID, verify your email with an OTP, then connect your MetaMask wallet — your voting identity, ready in minutes.

2

Cast your vote

Pick a candidate and confirm the transaction in MetaMask. Your vote is signed by your own wallet and written directly to the smart contract.

3

Verify the result

Results are tallied live from the blockchain and mirrored to a public results page — anyone can cross-check the count against the chain.

Why CivicChain

Built for trust, not just for demos

🔒 Double-vote proof

Enforced both by the smart contract's hasVoted mapping and a database-level unique constraint.

🧾 Full audit trail

Every login, vote, and admin action is logged with a timestamp for post-election review.

🪪 Role-based access

Clear separation between voter and admin permissions, enforced on every API route.

⚡ Zero-setup Windows build

CMake auto-detects MySQL & OpenSSL on Windows — no manual path editing required.
